# Capstone Rubric — 100 Points

- Problem framing and requirements — 8
- Python/code quality and modularity — 10
- Agent loop and state management — 10
- Tool design and permissions — 10
- RAG/grounding quality — 8
- Memory design — 6
- Planning and human approval — 8
- API and integration architecture — 8
- Reliability and recovery — 8
- Evaluation and testing — 10
- Security and responsible design — 8
- Observability, cost and operations — 4
- Documentation and demo — 2

A score below 70 does not pass. A security design that permits arbitrary unapproved side effects can be capped at 69 regardless of total points.
